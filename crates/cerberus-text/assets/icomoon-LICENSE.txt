Icon font: icomoon.ttf
======================

A small icon subset exported from IcoMoon (https://icomoon.io), supplied by the
project owner for the toolbar and cookie-manager icons.

IcoMoon's bundled free icon packs are licensed per-pack (commonly CC BY 4.0 or
GPL; see https://icomoon.io/#icons). Confirm the exact pack(s) used and record
the required attribution here before any public distribution of the binary.

Codepoints in use (Unicode Private Use Area), probed from this file:
  e984  reload / refresh
  e994  gear / settings
  ea38  arrow-left  (Back)
  ea34  arrow-right (Forward)
  ea0f  close (x)   (Stop / dismiss)
  e9ce  eye         (cookie "reveal value")
  e9ac  trash       (cookie "delete")

The file also contains other glyphs (book, users, database, …) in the
e91f–ea38 range that are not currently referenced.
