Icon font: icomoon.ttf — IcoMoon Free
=====================================

A subset of the "IcoMoon Free" icon set, exported via IcoMoon
(https://icomoon.io/app) and bundled for Cerberus's toolbar and cookie-manager
icons.

  Source: https://github.com/Keyamoon/IcoMoon-Free
  Changes: subset to the glyphs Cerberus uses; embedded as a binary asset.

Codepoints in use (Unicode Private Use Area):
  e984  reload / refresh
  e994  cog / settings
  ea38  arrow-left   (Back)
  ea34  arrow-right  (Forward)
  ea0f  cross / close (Stop, dismiss)
  e9ce  eye          (cookie "reveal value")
  e9ac  bin / trash  (cookie "delete")

The file also contains other IcoMoon Free glyphs (book, users, database, …)
that are not currently referenced.
