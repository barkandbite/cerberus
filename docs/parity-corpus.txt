# Rendering-parity corpus (RENDERING_PARITY_PLAN.md, Workstream 0).
#
# One page per line: `<name> <url> [width] [height]`. `scripts/parity.sh`
# mirrors each page to 127.0.0.1, renders it in headless Chrome (reference) and
# Cerberus, and scores the difference with `cerberus-app diff`. Lower RMSE is
# closer to Chrome; track the numbers over time to catch regressions and prove
# progress. Blank lines and `#` comments are ignored.
